The v4 README records the Robotiq STEP CAD and fingertip STEP CAD used to build
the model. RoboLab ports the v4 body/mesh transforms as one coordinate system;
it does not mix v4 geometry with the old ROS joint frames.

Menagerie's lower and upper pad boxes use scalar friction values `0.7` and
`0.6`, respectively. RoboLab binds them as separate Isaac materials: lower
`static=dynamic=0.7`, upper `static=dynamic=0.6`, both with zero restitution.
Only the two small pad collision instance roots are expanded to author these
bindings; the rest of the robot remains instanced. MuJoCo solimp and solref
parameters still have no direct URDF representation.

Physical reference
------------------

Robotiq, "2F-85 & 2F-140 Instruction Manual", 2018, section 6.2.3:
  2F-85 mass including coupling 0.900 kg; center of mass Z=57 mm;
  nominal TCP Z=171 mm.

RoboLab's Berkeley profile intentionally uses the dataset convention of
170 mm, not the manual's 171 mm nominal value.

Legacy provenance only
----------------------

The old visual/collision/xacro files in this directory came from the archived
ROS-Industrial Robotiq kinetic-devel visualization package:
https://github.com/ros-industrial-attic/robotiq/tree/kinetic-devel/robotiq_2f_85_gripper_visualization

They and the earlier `meshes/menagerie/` baseline are not referenced by the
canonical v4 URDF and must not be used as a physics source.
